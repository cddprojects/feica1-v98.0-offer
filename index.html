FAQs

Things people ask before they get started.

When and how do I get paid?
Your first payout is sent by Interac e-Transfer within 3 days of your first approved task. After that you're paid every Friday by Interac e-Transfer. There's no minimum payout and no holding period.
Can I do this alongside a full-time job?
That's how most people use it: evenings, lunch breaks and weekends. Check your employment contract or workplace policy first. Some employers require you to disclose outside work, and you should never do tasks on work time or work equipment.
What about taxes?
Income from tasks is generally considered self-employment or other income, so it should be reported on your tax return. We don't withhold tax from payouts. For anything specific to your situation, check with the CRA or a tax professional.
Who can apply?
Role Findly is open to Canadians who want flexible extra income from home. Setup is quick and simple: send your details, say hello to our team, and you can try your first task within a day or two. No résumé and no special equipment, just a phone or laptop.
Is there a fee to apply or get started?
No. Applying is free, onboarding is free, and tasks are free to start. You get paid for doing the work.
How much time do I need?
Most people start with 5–15 hours a week and adjust from there. There are no quotas or minimum hours. If you disappear for a week for a long weekend, a busy stretch at work or a family trip, nothing happens.
Do you support French speakers?
Oui. Our team can answer your questions in English or French over WhatsApp. Some task instructions are currently English-only.
