Privacy Policy

PIPEDA & CASL · Canada

Role Findly respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it, in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) and, where applicable, provincial privacy laws such as Alberta's and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Acts and Québec's Law 25.

1. Who we are

Role Findly ("we", "us", "our") operates this website for Canadian residents. Questions about privacy can go to our privacy contact at contact@rolefindly.com.

2. What we collect

When you submit a form on our website we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • If you used our estimator, the hours-per-week and experience level you selected

We do not ask for your Social Insurance Number, banking details or government ID through this website. If you apply and start earning with us, we'll collect payment details separately and securely when needed to pay you.

3. Why we collect it

  • To respond to your inquiry and assess which tasks suit you
  • To contact you by WhatsApp, text or email about the opportunity
  • To onboard and pay you if you choose to start earning with us
  • To improve our service

We won't use your information for other purposes without your consent.

4. Consent and CASL

By ticking the consent box on our forms you agree that we may contact you by WhatsApp, text message or email about this opportunity. You can withdraw consent at any time by replying "STOP" to any message, using the unsubscribe link in any email, or emailing us. Every commercial message we send identifies us and includes a way to unsubscribe.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We may share it with:

  • Our team members who handle onboarding and support
  • Service providers who help us run the business (for example, hosting, messaging and payment tools), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Authorities, if required by law

Some of these providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws.

6. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don't go further: up to 12 months, then securely deleted.
  • Active earners: for the length of your working relationship and up to 7 years afterwards for tax and accounting purposes.

7. How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards, including access controls, encrypted connections and staff confidentiality obligations, to protect your information from loss, theft and unauthorized access.

8. Your rights

You can ask to:

  • See the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Withdraw consent for us to use or contact you
  • Have your information deleted, subject to legal retention requirements

Email contact@rolefindly.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

This website doesn't currently use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Your browser may use basic session storage to make the page work.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date below shows the latest version.
